1. In one sentence, define inheritance.

Inheritance is having one class aquire the methods and
behaviors from another class.

2. What is the purpose of extends?

The "extends" keyword makes the class it was called on an extension of another class, inheriting the methods and behaviors from that class.

3. What is the difference between a superclass and a subclass?

The difference is that the superclass is the class that's being inherited, while the subclass is the class that inherits the superclass.
